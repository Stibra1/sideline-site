Sideline Spons

Every goal fills the team kitty

Per-goal pledges and online fundraising for your team. Family and friends choose an amount per goal, per win and for a clean sheet. Spons does the maths and the reminders, and the money goes straight to the team, never through us.

  1. 1

    Set up the team in 5 minutes

    The team leader or fundraising lead sets up Spons in the app or on the web, adds the matches and shares the team code.

  2. 2

    Family and friends choose an amount per goal

    Each supporter picks an amount per goal, win or clean sheet, with a cap so nobody gets a surprise.

  3. 3

    Pay after the match

    The team leader enters the result. Everyone is told what they give and pays the team directly.
