Sideline: Insights for iPhone

The match, in numbers.

Sideline: Insights is a stats app for coaches and parents in football and handball. Follow the whole team or one player live: tap the pitch or court where it happened, and get goals, shots, saves, heat maps and development over time.

Everything you need from the sideline.

Built for coaches and parents who want to see more than the score.

Football and handball

One global sport switch. Players can belong to several teams, across both sports.

Whole team or one player

When you start a match, choose whether to follow the whole team or one player live.

Live at the match

Tap where it happened: goals, shots, saves, misses, 7-metre throws/penalties, blocks, technical faults, steals, charges drawn, 2-minute suspensions, cards, corners, offsides and free kicks.

Heat maps and player cards

Season, last 5 or a single match. Attack and defence, shot heat maps on the pitch/court and the goal, and goal placement.

Development over time

Trend arrows and development charts show progress over time, by season, last 5 or single match.

After the match

Match summary with a stats table, team cards with combined stats, share as an image and export a CSV match report.

Your data stays on your iPhone.

Sideline: Insights does not collect personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

  • No account
  • No sign-in
  • No ads
  • No tracking
