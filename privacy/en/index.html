Privacy

Privacy Policy for the Sideline apps

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: None of the Sideline apps collect personal data. Everything stays on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Who we are

The Sideline apps are Sideline: Football, Sideline: Handball, Sideline: Insights, Sideline: Basketball and Sideline: Playbook, apps for coaches, team managers and parents. They are developed by Stian Braastad (private individual, Oslo, Norway). This policy covers all of the apps and explains how they handle information.

1. Summary

The apps do not collect personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The apps have no account, no sign-in, no third-party ads, no analytics and no tracking.

2. What is stored, and where

The apps store only what you enter yourself, in the app's own storage on your device:

  • Sideline: Football, Handball and Basketball (match boards): team name, opponent, player names and shirt numbers, formations and line-ups, matches, playing time, substitutions and events (such as goals, shots, saves, suspensions, fouls and cards).
  • Sideline: Insights (stats): teams and players (names and shirt numbers), matches and opponents, and events with their position on the pitch or court and in the goal. Stats, heat maps and charts are calculated on your device.
  • Sideline: Playbook (tactics board): the plays you create, with their names, pieces and shirt numbers or initials, lines and notes.

Settings such as sport and language are stored too. The apps do not send this data anywhere.

3. Sharing pictures and files

Some of the apps let you share a picture (such as a match summary or a play) or export a match report as a CSV file. The file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet, where you choose the recipient or app (for example email, Messages, Spond or Files). This only happens when you start it yourself. What you share may contain player names and stats, and how it is handled afterwards is up to the app or recipient you share it with. The developer receives nothing.

4. Information about other people, including children

Player names you enter may relate to other people, including children. This information stays on your device and under your control. We recommend entering only what you need, for example first names and shirt numbers, being careful about what you share, and following your club's guidelines.

5. Deletion

You can delete and edit what you have entered in the apps. Deleting an app from your device deletes all data that app has stored. If you use device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup on a computer), app data may be included in that backup. This is controlled by you and Apple, not by the developer.

6. Permissions

The apps do not ask for access to your camera, contacts, location or microphone, or for permission to track you, and none of them read your photos. If you choose “Save Image” in the share sheet in Sideline: Playbook, iOS asks whether the app may add a picture to your photo library. The app can then only add pictures, not see the ones you have.

7. Links to the App Store

Sideline: Playbook shows a small banner about the other Sideline apps. Tapping it opens the App Store. The banner collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer.

8. Apple

Downloads from the App Store are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Apple may give developers anonymised, aggregated figures (such as download numbers) and crash reports from users who have chosen to share them with developers. The developer cannot use these to identify you. Test versions through TestFlight follow Apple's TestFlight terms.

9. Contacting the developer

If you email support@sideline.no, I use your email address and message only to reply to you. The email is deleted once the matter is closed, unless you ask otherwise. The legal basis is legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

10. Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you have rights including access, rectification and erasure. Because the developer has no access to the data in the apps, you exercise these rights directly in the app or by deleting it. For questions about email you have sent, contact support@sideline.no. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet (www.datatilsynet.no).

11. This website

This website is published with GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement. The website loads no external fonts.

The website uses the Google tag (Google Ads) to measure how our ads for Sideline perform, for example whether someone arrived from an ad. On your first visit, we ask whether you accept Google Ads cookies. Until you answer, and if you decline, no such cookies are set; Google then only receives cookieless technical signals (Google Consent Mode). If you accept, Google may set cookies and process your IP address and information about your browser and visit, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The data may be transferred to the USA, on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Your choice is stored in your browser, and you can change it at any time.

The apps do not contain the Google tag or any other tracking.

12. Changes

If an app changes how it handles information, this policy is updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top changes.
