Match day, all in one app.

Sideline: Handball is a match board for handball coaches, team managers and parents, built with children's and youth handball in mind. Set your line-up, run the match clock, substitute with a drag and follow court time, goals, 7-metre throws, saves and 2-minute suspensions.

  • Line-ups and formations6+1, 5+1, 4+1 and 3+1 – pick a formation such as 6-0, 5-1 or 3-2-1, or place players freely and save your own.
  • Match clock and timeoutStart, Stop and Resume, Half-time and Full-time, and a one-tap team timeout that stops the clock.
  • Drag to substituteDrag from the bench to the court. Court time is tracked for you, and 2-minute suspensions count down on the player card.
  • Statistics and player cardsGoals, 7-metre throws, missed shots, saves, suspensions, cards and minutes for every player, with charts and shooting percentage.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no account and no sign-in.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The app works offline.

What happens if I delete the app?

All data the app has stored on your device is deleted. If you use device backups (for example iCloud Backup), app data may be included in that backup.

How do I delete a match?

On the home screen, swipe the match to the left and tap Delete, or press and hold the match and choose Delete. Confirm in the dialog. The match and all its statistics are deleted, and this can't be undone.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language (Norwegian, English, German, French, Spanish or Portuguese). You can also switch with the language button in the app.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@sideline.no. Please describe what you did, what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and attach a screenshot if you can.
